- demographic collapse: the baby boomers are hitting retirement age and will move from providing to the economic system, to extracting from it. Baby boomers have stayed in their homes longer than other generations, rather than moving out of the home they raised their kids in. These boomers have either no mortgage, or a cheap one. They will reach the point of frailty at some point in their life where their children either move into their homes and help take care of them and the house, they live in with their children in the children’s house, they hire a caregiver for at-home medical assistance, or they move to a nursing home. I believe, but am not certain, that we do not have the labor supply for at home care to be possible for most. I also believe, but am not certain, there are not enough nursing homes for the large wave of boomers. It is worth noting that this describes the US situation but the US is not nearly in as bad of shape as other countries like Japan, china, Korea, and others

In the USA, I believe boomers are the most inefficient and lowest-productivity workers. By and large, they don’t understand the new technologies or current economic landscape for younger generations. This excludes boomers who have been very successful and work at top companies. I imagine they have learned new technologies well. As Boomers are replaced in leadership roles across the country, companies will become much more productive

They will invest less, and in many cases they will end up paying for their elder parents, either for food, healthcare etc.
